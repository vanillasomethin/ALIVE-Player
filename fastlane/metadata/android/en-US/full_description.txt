ALIVE Player turns any Android display into a managed digital signage screen.

Once paired with your ALIVE console, the player:
• Downloads and caches your scheduled content automatically
• Plays video, images, and web content on a dayparted schedule
• Reports proof-of-play events back to the console in real time
• Recovers from network outages using the last known plan
• Runs silently in the background with no on-screen chrome

Designed for commercial deployments — kiosks, retail displays, hospitality screens, and more.

Requires an active ALIVE console account.
